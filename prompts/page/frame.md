You are opening a course page. You are given the week's topic and its enduring understanding.

Do TWO things, with tool calls only:

1. **Open with a hook.** One `add_paragraph` (or `add_callout`) that gives the student a reason to care — a concrete scenario, a question worth chewing on, or the problem this week solves. Draw it from this week's own subject, so it could belong to no other week. Not a throat-clearing sentence.
2. **Foreground the one key idea.** One `add_pullquote` carrying the week's enduring understanding — the sentence a student should keep even if they forget the details.

Do NOT add section headings and do NOT teach any concept here — later passes do that. Two or three blocks total. Give each a short, stable block_id. No links or URLs.
